## Reading Ek Skill Hai, Sirf Habit Nahi

Reading ko log casual samajhte hain, lekin effective reading ek skill hai jo practice se improve hoti hai. Alag situations me alag reading approach chahiye hoti hai — newspaper padhna aur textbook padhna same tarike se nahi hota.

---

## Kinds of Reading — Kab Kaunsa Use Karein

| Type | Kya Hai | Kab Use Karein | Speed |
|------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Skimming** | Poore text ko quickly overview karna, main idea pakadna | Newspaper headline, book ka overview lena | Bahut fast |
| **Scanning** | Specific information dhoondhna, poora text nahi padhna | Dictionary me word dhoondhna, timetable check karna | Fast, targeted |
| **Intensive Reading** | Detail me, dhyan se padhna, har word/concept samajhna | Textbook, technical manual, exam preparation | Slow, thorough |
| **Extensive Reading** | Lambe text ko general understanding ke liye padhna | Novel, magazine, general awareness ke liye | Medium, relaxed |

> Socho Aise — Skimming aisa hai jaise tum ek building ke bahar se dekh kar bata do "yeh ek office hai". Scanning aisa hai jaise ek specific room dhoondhna us building me. Intensive reading matlab poori building ka har room, corner explore karna.

Exam me "Kinds of Reading" poochha jaye toh table banao aur har type ka ek real-life example zaroor do — examiner ko practical understanding dikhta hai. Ye four types akele use nahi hote — ek achha reader situation ke hisaab se inhe combine karta hai, jaise pehle newspaper skim karke headline choose karna, phir us article ko intensively padhna.

## Causes of Reading Difficulties

| Cause | Explanation |
|-------|-------------|
| **Poor Vocabulary** | Words ka matlab na pata hone se comprehension ruk jaata hai |
| **Sub-vocalization** | Man hi man har word bolna (lip movement/inner voice) — speed kam kar deta hai |
| **Lack of Concentration** | Mind wander karna, distractions (phone, noise) |
| **Regression** | Baar baar peeche jaakar wahi lines dobara padhna |
| **Narrow Eye Span** | Ek time par sirf ek-do word hi read kar paana, poori line ka group nahi |
| **Lack of Purpose** | Bina goal ke padhna — pata hi nahi kyun padh rahe hain |
| **Unfamiliar Topic/Context** | Background knowledge na hone se samajhna mushkil hota hai |
| **Physical Factors** | Poor lighting, bad posture, tiredness |

> Warning — Sub-vocalization aur regression sabse common habits hain jo reading speed ko halve kar dete hain. In dono ko consciously control karna reading speed double kar sakta hai.

## Reading Strategies Aur Exercises

1. **Preview Before Reading** — Headings, sub-headings, pehla/last paragraph pehle dekh lo, ek mental map ban jaata hai.
2. **Set a Purpose** — Padhne se pehle decide karo — kya specific info chahiye ya general understanding.
3. **Chunk Reading** — Ek time me ek word nahi, 3-4 words ka group padhne ki practice karo — eye span badhta hai.
4. **Reduce Sub-vocalization** — Consciously koshish karo ki mann me words bolna band ho, seedha meaning grasp karo.
5. **Use a Pointer/Guide** — Finger ya pen se line ke saath follow karo — regression kam hoti hai.
6. **Practice Timed Reading** — Roz ek passage ko timer laga kar padho, WPM (words per minute) track karo.
7. **Build Vocabulary** — Naye words note karo, unka meaning aur usage seekho.
8. **Summarize After Reading** — Har paragraph/section ke baad 1-2 line me summarize karo — retention improve hoti hai.
9. **SQ3R Method** — Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review — ek structured reading technique hai textbooks ke liye.

> Example — SQ3R use karte hue: pehle chapter survey karo (headings dekho), phir headings ko questions me convert karo ("Barriers of Listening kya hain?"), phir read karo answer dhoondhne ke liye, phir apne words me recite karo, aur last me poora chapter review karo.

## Reading Speed Improve Karne Ke Practical Exercises

- Roz 15-20 minute ek fixed time slot me reading practice karo (newspaper, article, ya novel).
- Timer laga kar ek passage padho aur WPM calculate karo: `(Total words / Time in minutes)`.
- Comprehension check karo — passage padhne ke baad 3-4 questions khud se poocho.
- Gradually harder/longer texts try karo jaise speed aur comprehension dono improve ho.

> Yaad Rakho — Reading speed badhane ka goal comprehension ki keemat par nahi hona chahiye. Fast padhna tabhi useful hai jab samajh bhi utni hi achhi rahe — dono balance me improve karo.

## Comprehension Aur Vocabulary — Ek Doosre Se Judi Hui Skills

Reading comprehension seedhe vocabulary par depend karti hai. Do practical habits jo dono ko saath improve karte hain:

- **Context Clues Use Karo** — Agar koi word unfamiliar hai, use dictionary me turant dhoondhne ki jagah pehle sentence ke context se guess karne ki koshish karo.
- **Word Journal Maintain Karo** — Roz padhte waqt jo naye words mile, ek notebook/app me likho — word, meaning, aur ek apna sentence.
- **Reading Variety** — Sirf ek type ka material (jaise sirf textbook) mat padho — newspapers, novels, articles sab padho, isse vocabulary aur exposure dono badhta hai.

## Reading Aur Exam Preparation

MCA students ke liye reading strategies exam preparation me bhi kaam aati hain:

- Pehle poore chapter ko skim karke uska structure samajh lo.
- Important concepts intensive reading se dhyan se padho.
- Har section padhne ke baad apne words me short notes banao — ye passive reading ko active banata hai.
- Doubts ko turant highlight/note karo aur baad me clarify karo, flow todo mat.
