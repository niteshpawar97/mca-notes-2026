## Interview Kya Hota Hai

Interview ek formal conversation hai jisme employer candidate ki suitability evaluate karta hai — sirf technical knowledge nahi, balki communication, attitude, aur cultural fit bhi check hota hai. MCA students ke liye placement season me ye sabse critical skill hai.

---

## Types of Interviews

| Type | Description | Kya Expect Karein |
|------|-------------|---------------------|
| **One-on-One Interview** | Ek candidate, ek interviewer | Personal, conversational tone |
| **Panel Interview** | Ek candidate, multiple interviewers | Alag-alag angles se questions, thoda formal |
| **Telephonic Interview** | Phone par, usually screening round | Clear voice, no visual cues, concise answers zaroori |
| **Video/Virtual Interview** | Video call ke through | Good lighting, stable internet, professional background |
| **Group Interview** | Multiple candidates ek saath evaluate hote hain | Team skills aur individual standout dono important |
| **Technical Interview** | Subject-specific knowledge test | Coding, problem-solving, concepts se related questions |
| **HR Interview** | Personality, attitude, culture fit check karta hai | Behavioral questions, salary discussion, career goals |
| **Stress Interview** | Deliberately challenging/uncomfortable questions | Calm rehna, panic mat karo |

> Socho Aise — Technical interview tumhare "knowledge" ko test karta hai, jaise ek exam. HR interview tumhare "fit" ko test karta hai, jaise koi decide kar raha ho ki tum unke saath kaam karne ke liye sahi insaan ho ya nahi.

Exam me ye table format me likhna best hai — type, description, aur ek chhota tip har type ke liye. Aajkal companies inn types ko combine bhi karti hain, jaise ek virtual panel interview jisme technical aur HR dono rounds ek hi call me ho jaayein.

---

## Preparing for an Interview

**Before the Interview:**

- **Research the Company** — Company ka business, products, recent news, mission-vision padho.
- **Understand the Role** — Job description dhyan se padho, required skills note karo.
- **Practice Common Questions** — "Tell me about yourself", "Why should we hire you" jaise questions ready rakho.
- **Prepare Questions to Ask** — Interviewer se end me poochne ke liye 2-3 thoughtful questions ready rakho.
- **Organize Documents** — Resume, certificates, ID proof copies ready rakho.
- **Mock Practice** — Friends/family ke saath practice karo, ya mirror ke saamne.

**Dress Code:**

| Interview Type | Suggested Dress |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Corporate/Formal | Formal shirt-trouser/suit, muted colors |
| Startup/Casual | Smart casuals, still neat aur presentable |
| Virtual Interview | Formal from waist up (visible part), plain background |

> Warning — Chahe interview telephonic ya virtual ho, professionally dress karna aur seedha baithna posture aur confidence dono improve karta hai — casual mat lo isse.

## Structuring the Interview

Ek typical interview teen phases me chalta hai:

1. **Opening (Rapport Building)** — Greeting, handshake/smile, small talk. Pehla impression yahin banta hai — confidently enter karo, eye contact rakho.
2. **Main Body (Question-Answer)** — Technical aur behavioral questions. STAR method use karo behavioral answers ke liye:

```
S — Situation: Context describe karo
T — Task: Kya responsibility thi
A — Action: Tumne kya kiya
R — Result: Outcome kya raha
```

3. **Closing** — Interviewer questions poochne ka mauka dega ("Any questions for us?"). Thoughtful question poocho, thank you bolo, aur next steps confirm karo.

> Example — "Tell me about a time you worked in a team" ka STAR answer: "Mere final year project me (Situation), mujhe backend integrate karna tha teammates ke frontend ke saath (Task). Maine daily sync-up meetings organize kiye aur clear API documentation banayi (Action), jisse project deadline se pehle successfully complete hua (Result)."

> Yaad Rakho — Interview ka structure yaad rakhne ka simple formula: **Opening → Body → Closing** — jaise ek presentation ka structure hota hai, waise hi interview bhi ek "structured conversation" hai.

## Common Interview Questions — Preparation Areas

| Category | Sample Questions |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Personal** | Tell me about yourself; What are your strengths/weaknesses? |
| **Academic** | Explain your final year project; Which subject did you enjoy most? |
| **Technical** | Data structures, DBMS, OOP concepts related to your resume |
| **Behavioral** | Describe a time you faced a conflict in a team |
| **Situational** | What would you do if you disagreed with your manager? |
| **Company-specific** | Why do you want to join us? Where do you see yourself in 5 years? |

## After the Interview

- **Thank You Note** — Agar possible ho toh interviewer ko ek short thank-you email bhejo, professionalism dikhata hai.
- **Self-Reflection** — Kaunse questions difficult lage, unko note karo aur next interview ke liye practice karo.
- **Follow-up** — Agar reasonable time ke baad response na aaye, ek polite follow-up email bhej sakte ho.
