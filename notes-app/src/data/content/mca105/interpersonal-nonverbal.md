## Interpersonal Skills — Logon Ke Saath Kaam Karne Ki Kala

**Interpersonal skills** wo abilities hain jo humein doosre logon ke saath effectively interact karne me help karti hain — chahe wo colleagues ho, clients ho, ya friends. Workplace me technical skills se zyada kabhi kabhi interpersonal skills career growth decide karti hain.

**Key Interpersonal Skills:**

| Skill | Kya Hai |
|-------|---------|
| **Communication** | Clearly express karna aur actively sunna |
| **Empathy** | Doosre ke perspective/feelings ko samajhna |
| **Teamwork** | Collaborate karna, apni ego side me rakhna |
| **Conflict Resolution** | Disagreements ko constructively handle karna |
| **Adaptability** | Alag logon/situations ke saath adjust karna |
| **Positive Attitude** | Criticism ko gracefully lena, solution-oriented rehna |

Interpersonal skills ko "soft skills" ya "people skills" bhi kehte hain — ye technical knowledge ko complement karti hain, replace nahi.

## Negotiation Skills

Negotiation ek process hai jisme do ya zyada parties apne different interests ke beech ek mutually acceptable agreement dhoondhti hain.

**Win-Win Approach** — Best negotiation wo hoti hai jisme dono parties ko lage ki unhe kuch value mila hai (na ki ek side jeete, doosri haare).

**Steps of Negotiation:**

1. **Preparation** — Apni aur doosre party ki needs/priorities samjho, alternatives (BATNA) ready rakho.
2. **Discussion** — Dono sides apne points rakhein, active listening zaroori hai.
3. **Clarify Goals** — Interests identify karo (positions nahi) — "kya chahiye" vs "kyun chahiye".
4. **Negotiate towards Win-Win** — Options explore karo jo dono ko benefit de.
5. **Agreement** — Final terms par dono consent den, clearly document karo.
6. **Follow-up** — Agreement implement ho raha hai ya nahi, verify karo.

> Socho Aise — Negotiation ek pizza baatne jaisa hai. Agar dono log sirf "mujhe bada slice chahiye" par atke rahein toh fight hogi. Lekin agar pucha jaye "tumhe kyun bada slice chahiye" — shayad ek ko sirf cheese chahiye aur doosre ko crust — dono ko exactly wahi mil sakta hai jo chahiye, bina fight kiye.

Negotiation questions me "BATNA" (Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement) term likhna extra marks deta hai — ye batata hai ki agar deal na ho toh next best option kya hai.

## Non-Verbal Communication

Non-verbal communication me words ke bina message transfer hota hai — aur research kehta hai ki communication ka bada portion non-verbal hota hai.

| Signal | Common Meaning |
|--------|------------------|
| **Eye Contact** | Confidence, honesty, interest (zyada avoid karna disinterest/dishonesty dikhata hai) |
| **Facial Expressions** | Emotions directly show karti hain — smile, frown, raised eyebrows |
| **Posture** | Seedhi posture = confidence; slouching = disinterest/low energy |
| **Gestures** | Hand movements — emphasis ke liye ya kabhi unconscious nervousness |
| **Personal Space (Proxemics)** | Bahut close khada hona uncomfortable feel karata hai; culture-dependent |
| **Handshake** | Firm handshake = confidence; weak handshake = nervousness/disinterest |
| **Tone of Voice** | Emotion aur attitude reveal karta hai, chahe words neutral hon |
| **Crossed Arms** | Often defensiveness ya closed-off attitude dikhata hai |
| **Nodding** | Agreement ya active listening ka signal |

> Warning — Non-verbal signals culture-dependent hote hain — jo gesture ek country me normal hai wo doosri jagah offensive ho sakta hai. Formal/professional settings me hamesha neutral, respectful body language use karo.

> Example — Interview me achha non-verbal communication: seedhe baithna, interviewer se appropriate eye contact rakhna, halka smile, aur hands ko table par relaxed rakhna (fidget na karna).

## Workplace Etiquettes

- **Punctuality** — Meetings aur deadlines time par honi chahiye.
- **Respectful Communication** — Polite language, "please" aur "thank you" ka use.
- **Professional Dress Code** — Organization ke culture ke hisaab se appropriately dress karo.
- **Email Etiquette** — Clear subject line, professional tone, proofread before sending.
- **Meeting Etiquette** — Phone silent rakho, prepared aake baitho, unnecessarily interrupt mat karo.
- **Respect Personal Space & Boundaries** — Colleagues ki privacy aur time respect karo.
- **Constructive Feedback** — Criticism ko privately aur tactfully do, public me embarrass mat karo.

> Yaad Rakho — Etiquettes chhoti cheezein lagti hain lekin professional image banane me sabse zyada contribute karti hain — pehla impression aksar in hi cheezon se banta hai.

## Barriers to Interpersonal Communication

- **Ego Clashes** — Apni baat hi sahi maanna, doosron ke viewpoint ko consider na karna.
- **Lack of Trust** — Past experiences ke kaaran open communication na ho paana.
- **Stereotyping** — Kisi ko unki group identity (department, background) ke basis par judge karna, individual ke roop me nahi.
- **Poor Emotional Regulation** — Anger ya frustration me reactive comments karna jo relationships damage karte hain.

Inhe overcome karne ka best tareeka hai self-awareness — apni triggers pehchano, aur conversation me react karne se pehle ek pause lo.
