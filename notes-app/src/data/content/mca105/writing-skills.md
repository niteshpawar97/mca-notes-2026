## Effective Writing Ke Principles

Achhi writing sirf grammar sahi hone se nahi banti — ye clear thinking ka reflection hoti hai. Teen cheezein har achhi writing me common hoti hain:

| Principle | Matlab |
|-----------|--------|
| **Clarity** | Reader ko pehli baar me hi samajh aa jaye, koi confusion na ho |
| **Conciseness** | Kam se kam words me poori baat — unnecessary repetition avoid karo |
| **Coherence** | Ideas logically connected hon, ek se doosre point me smooth flow ho |
| **Correctness** | Grammar, spelling, punctuation sahi ho |
| **Courtesy** | Especially letters/emails me polite aur respectful tone ho |

> Yaad Rakho — Exam me "Effective Writing ke principles" poochha jaaye toh 5 C's yaad rakho: Clarity, Conciseness, Coherence, Correctness, Courtesy.

## Paragraph Aur Essay Writing — Quick Structure

**Paragraph Writing:**
- **Topic Sentence** — Paragraph ka main idea pehli line me.
- **Supporting Sentences** — Examples, facts, explanation.
- **Concluding Sentence** — Paragraph ko wrap up karo.

**Essay Writing:**
- **Introduction** — Topic introduce karo, thesis/main argument batao.
- **Body Paragraphs** — Har paragraph ek sub-point, examples ke saath.
- **Conclusion** — Summary aur final thought/opinion.

> Tip — Essay likhte time pehle ek rough outline banao (bullet points me) — introduction, 3 body points, conclusion. Isse writing structured rehti hai aur time bhi bachta hai.

## Report Writing

Report ek factual document hai jo kisi event, investigation, ya progress ko structured format me present karta hai.

**Structure of a Report:**

| Section | Contains |
|---------|----------|
| **Title** | Report ka clear, specific naam |
| **Introduction** | Purpose, background, scope |
| **Body** | Findings, data, analysis — headings/sub-headings ke saath |
| **Conclusion** | Summary of findings |
| **Recommendations** | Suggested actions (agar applicable ho) |

Report ek doctor ki diagnosis report jaisi hoti hai — pehle symptoms (findings), phir analysis, aur last me prescription (recommendation).

## Formal Letter Format

Formal letters — job application, complaint, request — ek fixed structure follow karte hain. Alignment aur spacing bhi important hoti hai.

```
                                                        Sender's Address
                                                        City, PIN Code
                                                        Date

To,
The [Designation],
[Company/Organization Name],
[Address]

Subject: [One line — purpose of the letter]

Sir/Madam,

Paragraph 1: Introduction — apna context/purpose clearly batao.

Paragraph 2: Main content — details, request, ya complaint explain karo.

Paragraph 3: Closing — action expected, thank you note.

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
[Contact Info]
```

Subject line ka example: "Subject: Application for the post of Software Trainee" — specific aur clear, generic nahi.

> Warning — Formal letter me "Yours faithfully" tab use hota hai jab receiver ka naam pata na ho ("Dear Sir/Madam" se start kiya ho). Agar naam pata ho ("Dear Mr. Sharma") toh "Yours sincerely" use karo. Exam me ye common mistake hai.

## Articles Aur Notices

**Article Writing** — Kisi topic par apna viewpoint present karna, newspaper/magazine ke liye. Structure: Heading → Byline (writer's name) → Introduction → Body (2-3 paragraphs) → Conclusion.

**Notice Writing** — Short, formal announcement kisi group ke liye (school, office, organization). Notice ek box me likha jaata hai.

```
                    [ORGANIZATION NAME]

                        NOTICE

Date: ___________

[HEADING/TITLE OF NOTICE]

Body: What, When, Where, Who — sab clearly mention karo
in 40-50 words. Purpose bilkul specific hona chahiye.


                                        [Name]
                                        [Designation]
```

Notice hamesha ek box (border) ke andar likha jaata hai exam me, aur usme Date, Heading, aur Issuing Authority ka naam/designation zaroor hona chahiye.

## Agenda & Minutes of Meeting

**Agenda** — Meeting se pehle circulate hone wala document, jisme discuss hone wale points list hote hain.

```
[ORGANIZATION NAME]
AGENDA

Meeting: [Purpose of meeting]
Date: ___________     Time: ___________     Venue: ___________

Items to be discussed:
1. Confirmation of previous minutes
2. [Agenda item 1]
3. [Agenda item 2]
4. Any Other Matter (AOB)
```

**Minutes of Meeting (MoM)** — Meeting ke baad likha jaata hai, jisme discussion aur decisions record hote hain.

```
[ORGANIZATION NAME]
MINUTES OF MEETING

Date: ___________     Time: ___________     Venue: ___________
Present: [List of attendees]
Absent: [List of absentees, if any]

1. Previous minutes confirmed.
2. [Item discussed] — Decision/Action taken — Responsible person.
3. [Item discussed] — Decision/Action taken — Responsible person.

Meeting concluded at: ___________
Next meeting date: ___________

                                        Recorded by: [Name]
```

> Socho Aise — Agenda ek "menu card" hai jo batata hai kya kya discuss hoga, aur Minutes ek "receipt" hai jo batata hai actually kya decide hua.

Minutes hamesha past tense me likhi jaati hain kyunki wo already ho chuki meeting ka record hain ("The committee decided...", "It was agreed that...").
